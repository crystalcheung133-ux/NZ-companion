# RC24.7.5 Implementation Report

## Scope

Corrected Guide behaviour and content rendering on the RC24.7.4 baseline.

## Implemented

- Trip Booking **Guide** opens the exact Guide card as an in-page modal.
- Day Timeline **Guide** opens the exact Guide card without leaving the day.
- **View Alternative** opens an in-page option list; selecting an option opens that Guide card.
- Closing Guide preserves the originating Trip Booking modal or Day page and its page state.
- Restaurant Guide cards retain Why Go, Suggested Dishes, booking advice and practical notes when those data exist.
- Activity/attraction Guide cards retain Why Go and practical information.
- Hotel Guide cards suppress check-in/reception/late-arrival filler.
- Southwark retains its useful Why Go and practical stay information.
- Trip accommodation details no longer render a Check-in notes section.
- UX contract updated to test the in-page modal contract rather than cross-page deep-links.
- Service-worker cache key advanced.
