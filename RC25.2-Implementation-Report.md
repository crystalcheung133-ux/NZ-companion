# NZ Companion RC25.2 — Implementation Report

## Scope

RC25.2 standardises the complete Guide system on the RC25.1.6 production baseline. It does not redesign the wider Travel Engine or change storage, Supabase, Expenses, Moments, booking authority, route generation or export architecture.

## Guide system implemented

- Audited 73 active Guide records.
- Accommodation: shared `Stay`, `Useful`, `Why Stay` structure; six confirmed stays aligned with canonical bookings.
- Dining: 31 restaurant/café records carry Trading Hours and use the shared Why Go → Suggested Dishes → Trading Hours → Booking → Good to Know order.
- Experiences: booking/session data, duration, meeting point and arrival requirements replace forced trading hours.
- Scenic/attraction cards: open-access places do not show hours; paid, gated or scheduled attractions may show Opening Hours and Admission.
- Shopping/markets: shared Why Stop → Trading Hours → Parking → Good to Know structure.
- Good to Know now appears only for action-changing information rather than repeating the Why statement.
- Standalone Guide pages no longer duplicate a generic Why Go section above the category-specific content.
- Shared Previous / Next and conditional Booking / Navigate actions remain in use.

## Phase 5 data updates

- FreshChoice Te Anau: Daily 7:00 AM–8:00 PM; breakfast, snacks, drinks and optional packed cruise lunch for Milford day.
