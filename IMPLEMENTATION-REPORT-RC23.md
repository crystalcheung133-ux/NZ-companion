# NZ Companion RC23 — Production Audit

## Implemented

- Guide → Stay now groups current stays by Day / Days.
- Confirmed stays show `CONFIRMED`; current primary choices needing final verification show `TO CONFIRM`.
- Booked backup accommodation is excluded from Guide → Stay and remains available in Trip → Accommodation.
- Guide stay rows open experiential Guide cards rather than booking-management cards.
- Stay Guide cards separate `Why we chose it`, `Facilities & practical fit`, and `Nearby & useful`.
- Stay Guide cards no longer duplicate booking price, payment, reference or cancellation data.
- Day 3 driving route and primary Google Maps route were corrected to follow:
  Peppers → Lake Pukaki Lookout → Mt Cook Alpine Salmon → Hooker Valley / White Horse Hill → Mt Cook Airport → Lindis Pass → Edgewater.
- Day 3 individual navigation targets were tightened for White Horse Hill car park and Mt Cook Glacier Guiding.
- Obsolete Astro Cafe cancellation commentary was removed from the live Day 3 timeline.
- Sudima Guide copy now identifies it as the Day 4 primary choice requiring reconfirmation, without commercial booking details.

## Deliberate ownership rule

- Timeline: what happens and in what order.
- Guide: why the place or experience was selected, facilities, practical fit and nearby context.
- Trip Info: booking reference, price, cashback, payment, cancellation, check-in and operational booking management.
